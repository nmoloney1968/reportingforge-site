Example build: Cash, sales, and operations visibility

Example 1: Cash, sales, and operations in one dashboard

This example is for an SMB operator who is tired of hunting across tools to answer basic weekly questions like Do we have enough cash, what is actually closing, and what needs attention right now.

Prefer the quick skim? Open the TL;DR version.

You connect a few common systems, clean the data once, and then refresh on demand or on a schedule.

Step 1: Connect the sources

Typical sources:

  • QuickBooks for invoices, payments, expenses, and cash position.
  • HubSpot for deals, pipeline stages, close dates, and owners.
  • Google Sheets for targets, product mapping, or a small override table.

You can start with exports (CSV) and upgrade to API pulls when you want the refresh to be hands-off.

Step 2: Build clean tables and consistent KPIs

Clean, reusable tables:

  • qb_invoices, qb_payments, qb_expenses
  • hs_deals
  • targets (from Sheets)

Operator-grade KPIs:

  • Cash runway (7, 30, 90 days based on recent burn).
  • Accounts receivable aging (0 to 30, 31 to 60, 61 to 90, 90+).
  • Collections rate (payments received divided by invoiced).
  • Pipeline health (value by stage, expected close by week).
  • Forecast vs target (weekly and monthly).

AI helps write the pull scripts and measures. You verify definitions so the numbers match reality.

Step 3: Publish a dashboard that answers operator questions

Questions you can answer in under 5 minutes:

  • Do we have enough cash for the next 30 days?
  • Which customers are slowing down payments?
  • Is pipeline moving, or just sitting?
  • Are we on track for the month, and what lever should we pull?

Suggested layout:

  • Top row: Cash, AR, Forecast vs Target (3 KPI cards).
  • Middle: AR aging chart, Pipeline by stage, Deals closing this month.
  • Bottom: Overdue invoices table with owner and next action.

What you end up with

  • A refreshable dashboard that replaces weekly copy-paste reporting.
  • One place to manage KPI definitions, so you stop arguing about numbers.
  • A fast change loop where you can add a metric in minutes.
