Privacy Lightweight and straightforward

Privacy

Reporting Forge is a pre launch project. There is no product to log into and no in app telemetry. This site collects only a small amount of information needed to run a normal web presence and handle email from people who want to stay in touch.

Website analytics and logs

The site is served through a standard hosting provider and may sit behind a CDN such as Cloudflare. These services automatically collect basic request and error logs. They typically include information like IP address, browser type, operating system, and the pages you visit.

These logs are used for security, performance, and simple traffic understanding. They are not used to build individual marketing profiles and are not sold to third parties.

Cookies

The static site itself does not set tracking cookies. Any cookies that appear will generally be from the hosting or CDN layer used to keep the site running smoothly, and they are used for things like routing and security.

Email and early access

If you email hello@reportingforge.com or choose to join the early access list for the book and lab, your contact details are used only to communicate about Reporting Forge and related learning materials.

Your email address will not be sold to third parties. You can ask to be removed from any list at any time by sending a short note to the same address.

Third party services

Standard infrastructure providers may be involved in delivering email or hosting files, for example an email provider and cloud storage. These providers will process your data only to the extent required to deliver those services.

Future changes

If Reporting Forge evolves into a hosted product with user accounts or more advanced analytics, the privacy notice will be updated before those changes go live. The intent is to stay clear, minimal, and respectful about what is collected and why.

If you have questions about this privacy notice, email hello@reportingforge.com.
