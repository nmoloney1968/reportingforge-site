Ideal early teams

Teams that already automate parts of their reporting with Python or SQL, have clear recurring outputs, and feel the pain of manual glue work between tools.

Helpful context to include
  • Rough description of your current reporting stack.
  • How often you ship recurring reports and to whom.
  • Where things tend to break or require late night fixes.

Short, concrete examples are more useful than long essays.
