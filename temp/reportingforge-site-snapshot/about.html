About Why Reporting Forge exists

Reporting that feels boring, in a good way.

Reporting Forge is being built for teams who live in Python, SQL, and spreadsheets, but still spend too much time hand assembling client reports. The goal is simple: define reporting workflows once, make them observable, and let the platform run them on a schedule without late night glue work.

Under the hood, Reporting Forge is a thin automation layer - not a new BI tool. It sits beside your existing stack, orchestrates data pulls and checks, renders templates, and delivers outputs in the formats stakeholders already expect.

  • Keep using your existing databases, APIs, and file drops - Reporting Forge orchestrates, it does not replace.
  • Every report run is version controlled and auditable so you can see what changed and why.
  • Outputs stay flexible - PDFs, slide decks, CSVs, dashboards, or custom handoffs.
Built by someone who has spent years wiring up operational reports for mines, projects, and finance teams - and wants that work to feel calm and predictable instead of last minute.

What this project is

Reporting Forge focuses on the practical last mile of reporting: pulling data, running checks, rendering templates, and delivering outputs. It is meant to be light enough that you can keep most of your logic in normal Python modules and existing tools.

What this project is not

It is not another dashboard tool or a full data platform. It assumes you already have storage, warehouses, and BI in place, and avoids asking you to rebuild those.

Who it is for

Data folk, analysts, and operations people who already script in Python or SQL and want a light way to turn those scripts into repeatable reporting workflows without standing up a whole internal platform from scratch.

FAQ

Is this another ELT tool?
No. It orchestrates the last mile of reporting, not raw data pipelines.

Is this a hosted platform?
Early versions will be self-hosted or containerized. Hosted may come later.

Does it replace Airflow?
No. It is intentionally far lighter and focused only on reporting workflows.

Do I need a big team to run it?
No. A single analyst can run a whole reporting suite on it.
