Privacy notice - pre launch

This site is currently a simple static landing page with email routing. There are no user accounts, logins, or product telemetry yet. This privacy notice describes what is collected in this early stage.

What this site collects

  • Standard web server logs provided by Cloudflare such as IP address, user agent, and requested pages.
  • Emails that you send to Reporting Forge addresses such as [email protected] or [email protected].

How this information is used

  • To reply to you when you reach out about early access or questions.
  • To understand roughly how many people are visiting the site and which pages are viewed most.

What is not happening

  • No third party ad tracking pixels are installed.
  • No sale of any personal information.
  • No automated decision making or profiling based on site visits.

Contact

If you have questions about how your information is handled, email [email protected] which currently routes to the same inbox as other Reporting Forge mail.

This notice will evolve as the product grows, especially once user accounts, authentication, and hosted workflows are introduced.
