Changelog Milestones on the way to launch

What is changing as Reporting Forge takes shape.

This page tracks the evolution of Reporting Forge from an idea into a tool people can use. Early entries focus on groundwork, architecture, and foundations.

2025-11-22 - Initial public site

- reportingforge.com domain established and secured (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, DNSSEC).
- Cloudflare Pages deployed with a structured multi-page site.
- Email routing, aliases, and send-as configured.
- Early positioning and narrative drafted publicly.

Planned next steps

- Define initial workflow runner architecture.
- Identify first internal proof-of-concept workflow.
- Build minimal configuration format for workflows.
- Create first template renderer and first delivery connector.

This will grow slowly and honestly over time. When the platform becomes installable, the changelog will switch to version-based releases.
