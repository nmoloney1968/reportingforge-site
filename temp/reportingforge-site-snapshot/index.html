Example workflow

Define a small Python script that pulls usage data, enriches it, and hands it to a report template. Reporting Forge schedules it, renders the output, and ships it to your clients or leadership without you touching Excel at 1am.

Tech notes

Designed to sit beside your existing stack: Git, SQL, Python, and your favourite BI tool. Think of it as the orchestration and formatting layer for everything you already know.

Environment name: reportingforge-prod

What Reporting Forge is NOT
  • Not a BI tool. It doesn’t replace Tableau, Power BI, or Looker.
  • Not a data pipeline framework. It assumes your data already exists somewhere.
  • Not a heavy workflow engine. It’s intentionally small and Python-first.
  • Not a rigid SaaS with one “right way.” You own your scripts, templates, and logic.
  • Not another complex layer to manage. It aims to make reporting boring and predictable.
