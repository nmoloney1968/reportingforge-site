Early access

Join the early access list

The first edition of Reporting Forge is a 30 day plan that teaches you how to use AI, Python, APIs, and Power BI together to automate a real report from end to end - without needing to become a full-time developer.

Early access is for people who want to be part of the very first cohort using the book and the SkyLink Test Network sandbox in their own business.

Who early access is for

  • Owners and managers who are buried in monthly reporting and board packs.
  • Analysts who want a safe, guided way to bring AI and Python into their workflow.
  • Team leads who want to prove an automation concept before rolling out anything bigger.

What you get as an early reader

  • The full digital copy of the book as soon as the early access edition is ready.
  • Access to the SkyLink Test Network sandbox API and example datasets.
  • A practical prompt library you can copy, paste, and adapt for your own reports.
  • Updates on additional bundles (checklists, templates, example workflows) as they are released.

How it works

Early access is deliberately small. The plan is to work with a limited number of readers, gather feedback on the 30 day sequence, and refine the prompts and examples before a wider release.

There is no obligation or payment to join the list – it simply means you will hear first when the early access edition is available.

To join the early access list, use your normal email tools to send a short note to early@reportingforge.com with:
  • your name,
  • a couple of lines about your reporting environment, and
  • one workflow you would love to automate.
This keeps things intentionally lightweight: no forms, no funnels – just a short human note so we can keep the group focused and relevant.
