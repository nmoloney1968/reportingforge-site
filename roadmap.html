Roadmap

Where Reporting Forge is heading

Reporting Forge is intentionally small: one book, one sandbox, and a focused set of examples that help you automate a real workflow. The roadmap is about deepening that, not turning it into a sprawling platform.

Phase 1 – First edition and sandbox

Book launch and SkyLink Test Network

  • Release of the first digital edition of the 30 day AI-first reporting book.
  • Public SkyLink Test Network sandbox API at https://reportingforge.com/api/skylink/.
  • Companion resources: prompt library, worksheets, and basic checklists.
Phase 2 – Bundles and deeper examples

Optional upgrade paths

  • Extra example workflows for common scenarios (board packs, operational KPIs, usage analytics).
  • Power BI template files that mirror the main examples from the book.
  • Extended SkyLink datasets for teams who want more scale for testing.
Phase 3 – Teams and shared patterns

Helping small teams standardise reporting

  • Guides for adapting the prompts and patterns to team environments.
  • Suggested structures for internal “reporting playbooks” that combine AI, Python, APIs, and BI.
  • Selective case studies where teams are willing to share anonymised workflows.
Out of scope

What Reporting Forge is not planning to become

  • Another hosted BI platform or dashboard product.
  • A fully-managed “black box” automation service.
  • A replacement for your existing data warehouse or analytics stack.

The goal is to stay close to the original promise: teach people with real reporting problems how to use AI and simple tools to automate one real workflow - and then repeat that success in other parts of the business.
