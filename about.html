About Why Reporting Forge exists

Reporting that feels boring, in a good way.

Reporting Forge exists for people who are tired of fragile, manual reporting and want to learn how to automate it without becoming full time engineers. The goal is simple - help you move from copy and paste chaos to a calm, repeatable reporting stack you understand.

The first version of Reporting Forge is not a hosted platform. It is a book and a set of labs that teach you how to build the last mile of reporting yourself using stock Python, AI tools, and Power BI. You will work on real reports you already ship and make them less painful, step by step.

Under the hood, the book focuses on four things:

  • Pulling data from the sources you already have - exports, APIs, and simple databases.
  • Running checks and small pieces of logic in Python so errors show up early and clearly.
  • Producing clean, well named output files that tools like Power BI and Excel can use.
  • Scheduling those scripts so reports refresh without last minute manual work.

AI shows up throughout as a helper. You will use an AI assistant to draft and debug code, but the book still expects you to understand the decisions you are making. The aim is not click and hope automation. It is to build a small reporting system you trust.

Built by someone who has spent years wiring up operational reports for mines, projects, and finance teams, and who wants that work to feel calm and predictable instead of last minute and stressful.
This book is the distillation of those lessons into a path you can follow on evenings and weekends without a dedicated data team.

What this project is

Reporting Forge is a learning environment and lab for the practical last mile of reporting: pulling data, running checks, shaping outputs, and delivering them to the people who need them. It is light enough that most logic lives in normal Python modules and existing tools.

What this project is not

It is not another dashboard tool or a full data platform. You do not rebuild your warehouse or migrate every report. You start with a few important recurring reports and make them more reliable using tools you can maintain yourself.

Who it is for

Analysts, operations leaders, and domain experts who:

  • Ship recurring reports or dashboards today.
  • Are willing to write some Python with help from AI, even if they would not call themselves developers.
  • Care more about dependable outputs than about shiny stacks.
Why a book and lab first

Before any hosted platform exists, it is important to prove that the patterns are teachable and useful. The book and lab are the best way to do that. They also give you real skills even if you never use a future hosted version of Reporting Forge.
