Example build: Service performance and retention risk

Example 3: Service performance that protects renewals

This example is for an SMB operator who wants a simple answer to: Are we meeting our service promise, and which customers are at risk because support is struggling?

Prefer the quick skim? Open the TL;DR version.

You connect your ticket system to your CRM, apply simple SLA targets, and make risk visible before churn shows up in revenue.

Step 1: Connect the sources

Typical sources:

  • Zendesk or Freshdesk for tickets, tags, priorities, and timestamps.
  • CRM (HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce) for account tier, owner, renewal date, and ARR.
  • Spreadsheet for SLA targets and escalation rules.

Even a small team can benefit from a daily refresh and a single backlog view.

Step 2: Build clean tables and consistent KPIs

Clean, reusable tables:

  • tickets, ticket_events
  • accounts (from CRM)
  • sla_targets (from your sheet)

Operator-grade KPIs:

  • SLA compliance for first response and resolution.
  • Median first response and median resolution by priority.
  • Backlog (open tickets) and trend.
  • Top categories driving volume and rework.
  • At-risk accounts flagged by ticket intensity and SLA misses.

You are not building an enterprise ITSM platform. You are building a simple view that helps you protect customer trust.

Step 3: Publish a dashboard that answers operator questions

Questions you can answer in under 5 minutes:

  • Are we meeting our service promise this week?
  • What is breaking right now, and what category is driving it?
  • Which customers are at risk due to support pain?
  • Where should we add capacity or fix the product?

Suggested layout:

  • Top row: SLA %, Median first response, Median resolution, Open backlog.
  • Middle: Volume by category, SLA misses by priority, trend lines.
  • Bottom: At-risk accounts table with owner and renewal date.

What you end up with

  • A refreshable service dashboard that management can trust.
  • A practical way to spot churn risk early.
  • A clean path to add more signals later (NPS, product telemetry, refunds).
